# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Smart School Scheduling a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report issues via the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review and take appropriate action.
